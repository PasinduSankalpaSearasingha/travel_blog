Upper Bambarakanda

The term "Bambarakanda upper" refers to the hike to the top of Bambarakanda Falls, the highest waterfall in Sri Lanka. The trek takes you above the main cascade, where you can see the smaller waterfall and pool that feed the larger falls and enjoy panoramic views of the surrounding area.


Dediyagala

Dediyagala is part of a forest complex and biosphere reserve in southern Sri Lanka, located in the Matara and Galle districts. It is one of the last remaining large rainforests in the country, alongside the Sinharaja Forest Reserve
